# Simultaneous contact authoring

`ABattleObject::ContactPriority` is the authored contact priority of a battle object: an integer, default zero, set through `SetContactPriority(Priority)`. Higher values resolve first when several contacts reach one target in the same frame. It persists through hit toggles and fighter state changes until the object is reset or reused, and it survives `SaveGameState`/`LoadGameState`. `AddBattleObject`, `ResetObject`, `EnableHit`, `DeactivateObject`, `SaveGameState` and `LoadGameState` keep their engine meanings.

`FNSE006Battle` is a neutral authoring fixture. It builds one synthetic world with two fighters (`P1` at x = -50000 facing right, `P2` at x = +50000 facing left, both on the ground at y = 0, 10000 health, combo rate 50) and a pool of four battle objects. `Step(Input1, Input2)` runs the real battle frame. `Reset()` returns the same world to its constructed shape: objects reset, fighters back in idle with fresh health, flags, hit data, armor, priority and contact history, trace, state entries, snapshots and pending actions cleared.

`Spawn(Owner, Key, X, Y, Slot)` activates a pooled object for a fighter with an authored key (use values of 2 and above; fighters are keyed 0 and 1), facing the owner's way, with a 4x4 hurtbox. `Slot` forces the lowest free pool slot at or above that index. `Strike(Attacker, Damage, Priority, AttackLevel, Hitstop)` replaces the object's hit data and makes it an active mid strike with the given hitstop (default 3) in both its normal and counter hit data, no hitstop modifier, hitstun 10, 100 percent initial and forced proration, minimum damage 0, ground-normal reactions, counter-hit data equal to normal-hit data, and the given priority; it adds no boxes, and it sets the attack flags directly rather than through `EnableHit`. A counter hit splits hitstop by role, as the game does: the attacker is assigned its strike's normal hitstop, and only the victim takes the strike's counter-hit hitstop. Other hit data and armor values are written to the public fields after `Strike` or `Armor`. `Boxes(Object, Boxes)` replaces the object's boxes; `Box(Width, Height, Type, X, Y)` builds one; left-facing objects mirror box X offsets. `Armor(Target, Hits, bStrikes, bProjectiles, bDisableIncomingHit)` gives guard-type armor with a hit budget. `Guard(Player, bEnabled)` lets a fighter block while it holds away from the opponent (P1 holds `INP_Left`, P2 holds `INP_Right`). `Move(Object, DeltaX, DeltaY)` applies a one-frame displacement the next time the object's state script executes, and `Defer(Object, Action)` runs an arbitrary action at that same moment; for a fighter entering its reaction that is on entry, after the engine has cleared its flags, hit data and armor. `OnHit(Attacker, Action, FollowUpKey, X, Y)` runs `DisableHit`, `Deactivate` or `SpawnFollowUp` from the attacker's hit callback; a follow-up belongs to the attacker's owner, uses `Strike` defaults and a 4x4 hit box, and `SpawnFollowUp` fires once, then clears itself. `OnHitDo(Attacker, Action)` runs an arbitrary action once, from the attacker's next hit callback. `InvulnerableOnReceive(Player, Frames)` makes the fighter strike-invulnerable for `Frames` frames from its receive-hit callback. `Snapshot()` saves the engine's battle snapshot together with the fixture's own tables and returns a handle; `Restore(Handle)` loads it back and clears the trace and state entries.

`Trace` records callbacks as `{Kind, Actor, Other, TargetHealth}`: `Hit`, `CounterHit`, `Block` and `HitOrBlock` fire on the attacker with `Other` = its attack target; `Receive` fires on the target with `Other` = its attack owner; `TargetHealth` is the health of the fighter on the receiving end when the callback fired, -1 when that end is not a fighter. `Count(Kind, Actor, Other)` counts entries with -1 as a wildcard. `StateEntries` lists every fighter state entry in the order it happened as `{Player, State}`. `KeyOf`, `Find` and `Script` map between keys, objects and their state scripts. Fighter state scripts re-register the recorders and restore the fighter's cel whenever they execute, so authored boxes survive hit reactions. A fighter entering a hitstun state applies the stun of the hit it received on entry, as the game's universal hitstun states do, and takes no pushback.
